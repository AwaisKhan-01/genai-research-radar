### SynCity 3000: Bootstrapping Scene-Scale 3D Diffusion
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05392v1)

**The TL;DR:** SynCity 3000 is a framework that generates large, coherent, and detailed 3D scenes by adapting existing image-to-3D generators to operate convolutionally on scene blueprints.

**Architectural Innovation:** The core innovation is adapting an existing image-to-3D generator to function as a convolutional operator, enabling it to process larger "dimetric images" (scene blueprints derived from prompts) and generate entire 3D scenes rather than just single assets. This allows for scalable, patch-based 3D generation. The framework also includes a novel synthetic data engine to generate scene-scale 3D data, addressing a significant training data scarcity problem.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This approach offers a powerful solution for rapidly generating complex, globally coherent 3D environments and assets for applications like virtual worlds, gaming, simulations, and architectural visualization. By making existing single-asset generators scalable via a convolutional approach, it bypasses the need for entirely new large-scale 3D models and facilitates the creation of "arbitrarily sized and complex" scenes from text prompts or simple layouts. Furthermore, the proposed synthetic data engine provides a practical method to overcome the perennial challenge of acquiring sufficient 3D scene data for training robust GenAI models, accelerating development in data-starved domains.

### Deform360: A Massive Multi-view Visuotactile Dataset for Deformable World Models
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05390v1)

**The TL;DR:** This paper introduces Deform360, a massive multi-view visuotactile dataset and a novel 3D tracking pipeline for deformable objects, enabling systematic benchmarking and development of robot world models.

**Architectural Innovation:** The primary architectural innovation is a "novel markerless visuotactile 3D tracking pipeline." This system captures dense geometry and motion from 41 surround-view cameras and bimanual tactile grippers to generate the Deform360 dataset. This is an architectural change in the data acquisition and processing infrastructure for deformable objects, rather than modifications to existing generative AI model architectures like DiT or U-Net.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This dataset and its derived insights are critical for teams building Generative AI pipelines for robotic manipulation, especially when dealing with deformable objects (e.g., soft robotics, handling food, medical devices, logistics of flexible goods). It provides the high-quality, diverse data needed to train and validate more robust and generalizable world models, leading to improved robot planning and execution for complex tasks. The systematic evaluation of 2D vs. 3D world models offers practical guidance on architectural choices, helping engineers optimize for either scalability (2D models) or structural fidelity (3D models) based on product requirements. The markerless 3D tracking pipeline itself presents a valuable blueprint for building similar advanced data collection systems for other challenging real-world scenarios.

### InFlux++: Real and Synthetic Data for Estimating Dynamic Camera Intrinsics
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05389v1)

**The TL;DR:** InFlux++ provides large-scale synthetic and real datasets with diverse ground truth camera intrinsics to significantly improve the accuracy of per-frame intrinsic estimation for dynamic videos.

**Architectural Innovation:** No novel model architecture is proposed in this paper. The core innovation lies in establishing a robust data architecture for training and evaluation: a large-scale procedurally generated synthetic dataset (InFlux++ Synth) offering diverse, per-frame ground truth intrinsics and associated scene information, alongside an extended real-world benchmark (InFlux++ Real) to evaluate model performance under a wider range of dynamic conditions.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This work directly enables the development of more robust Generative AI pipelines that rely on 3D scene understanding from video (e.g., NeRFs, implicit representations, 3D asset generation from video). By providing high-quality, diverse training data for dynamic camera intrinsics, engineers can:
1.  **Improve 3D Reconstruction Quality:** Enhance the accuracy and stability of 3D reconstructions from "in-the-wild" video footage by accurately accounting for dynamic camera changes like zoom and focus, leading to higher quality GenAI outputs.
2.  **Reduce Manual Calibration:** Minimize the need for time-consuming manual camera calibration or fixed-intrinsics assumptions, streamlining the data ingestion pipeline for consumer-grade video content into 3D GenAI systems.
3.  **Boost Model Robustness:** Train intrinsics prediction models that generalize better to real-world scenarios, making foundational 3D GenAI components more resilient to varied input data and thus improving overall system reliability and inference quality.
4.  **Leverage Synthetic Data Paradigms:** Apply the procedural generation approach for synthetic datasets to other complex GenAI tasks where acquiring diverse, perfectly annotated real-world ground truth is a significant bottleneck.
