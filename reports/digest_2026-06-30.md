### Reweighting Framewise Attention in Video Transformers for Facial Expression Understanding
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30611v1)

**The TL;DR:** MiRA is a plug-in attention redistribution method for Vision Transformers that enhances their focus on subtle, localized facial dynamics in videos by reweighting existing attention maps without adding trainable parameters.

**Architectural Innovation:** This framework introduces a novel *framewise attention redistribution mechanism* that plugs into existing ViT backbones, specifically modifying how self-attention weights are interpreted and applied. It derives frame-level importance and intra-frame concentration statistics from the existing self-attention maps to redistribute attention toward salient spatio-temporal facial cues. Architecturally, it presents two modes: an "exact mode" based on *post-softmax attention redistribution*, and a more efficient "flashLite mode" which is a *pre-softmax approximation* designed to integrate this marginal redistribution directly *into FlashAttention kernels*, thus modifying the attention computation at a low-level optimization.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This technique offers a direct pathway to improve the realism and expressiveness of GenAI models dealing with video, particularly for generating human-like content (e.g., virtual avatars, digital humans, expressive video synthesis) where capturing nuanced facial expressions or fine-grained movements is critical. As a "plug-in" module that introduces "no additional trainable parameters," it can be integrated into existing ViT-based GenAI pipelines *without requiring extensive model retraining or architectural overhauls*. The `flashLite mode`, by integrating into FlashAttention, suggests that this quality improvement can be achieved *efficiently*, potentially even maintaining or improving inference speed for models already using FlashAttention, offering a high-quality output for subtle details without a proportional increase in computational cost.

### UnfoldArt: Zero-Shot Recovery of Full Articulated 3D Objects from Text or Image
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30608v1)

**The TL;DR:** This paper introduces a novel agent-based system combined with a video generative prior to reconstruct complete, articulated 3D objects, including hidden geometry, from single text or image inputs.

**Architectural Innovation:** The core innovation is a "debate-driven agentic approach" that orchestrates high-level agents (leveraging vision-language and video models for semantic and motion reasoning) and low-level agents (estimating articulation parameters). These agents engage in a two-round structured debate, grounded by a freely generated video prior, which is then explicitly used to simulate the object's articulation to reveal and reconstruct occluded interiors and geometry. This represents a system-level architectural innovation for 3D reconstruction, rather than a modification to a single deep learning model.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This approach provides a powerful tool for automated, zero-shot generation of high-fidelity, articulated 3D models with inferred internal structures, directly from text prompts or single images. This significantly reduces the manual effort and time required for 3D content creation in domains such as game development, virtual/augmented reality, robotics simulation, and embodied AI, while improving the completeness and realism of generated 3D assets.
