### Wat3R: Underwater 3D Geometry Learning without Annotations
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08772v1)

**The TL;DR:** Wat3R reconstructs accurate 3D geometry from unlabeled underwater video by adapting existing models via a semi-supervised teacher-student framework and a novel cross-view consistency loss, circumventing the critical challenge of acquiring labeled underwater data.

**Architectural Innovation:** The core innovation is a *cross-domain semi-supervised learning framework* that employs a *teacher-student architecture* to adapt feed-forward 3D reconstruction models from clear air environments to complex underwater conditions. This framework is augmented by a novel *cross-view consistency loss* designed to exploit geometric cues across multiple views, effectively compensating for optical degradation caused by water attenuation and scattering.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This approach offers significant value for GenAI pipelines that either consume 3D geometry as input, generate 3D assets, or operate in simulation environments requiring accurate 3D representations. The primary benefit is the *elimination of costly and impractical underwater 3D annotations*, drastically reducing development cycles and data acquisition overhead for new underwater applications (e.g., training GenAI models for underwater robotics, environmental monitoring, or virtual scene generation). The robust 3D reconstruction, even in poor visibility, ensures higher quality and more reliable geometric data, which can then improve the fidelity of generated content or the accuracy of models reliant on such inputs, without directly increasing inference speed but by providing superior training data or real-time environmental understanding.

### LongE2V: Long-Horizon Event-based Video Reconstruction, Prediction, and Frame Interpolation with Video Diffusion Models
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08770v1)

**The TL;DR:** LongE2V leverages fine-tuned video diffusion models and novel temporal stabilization techniques to reconstruct, predict, and interpolate high-quality, long-horizon video from sparse event camera data.

**Architectural Innovation:** They augment a foundational video diffusion model with "Autoregressive Unrolling" and "Adaptive Context Switching" to mitigate temporal drift in long sequences. For precise bidirectional consistency during frame interpolation, they introduce "Reencoding Alignment with Cross Residual Correction," and for input robustness across varying sensor resolutions, they incorporate "Event Voxel Density Augmentation."

**Engineering Takeaway:** This approach enables the generation of high-quality, temporally coherent video from power-efficient and high dynamic range event camera data, crucial for real-time GenAI applications in autonomous systems, robotics, and surveillance operating in challenging environments (e.g., low light, high speed). The data efficiency from fine-tuning a foundational model, coupled with robust long-sequence handling and superior interpolation, can significantly enhance perception pipelines and video generation capabilities in production GenAI systems utilizing specialized sensors.

### OPSD-V: On-Policy Self-Distillation for Post-Training Few-Step Autoregressive Video Generators
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08766v1)

**The TL;DR:** OPSD-V is a self-distillation technique that post-trains fast autoregressive video generators using real video data as dense temporal supervision to improve long-horizon consistency and motion dynamics without altering their inference speed.

**Architectural Innovation:** The innovation lies in a novel *on-policy self-distillation training paradigm* applied to existing autoregressive video generators. It introduces a teacher-student setup where the student follows the exact inference rollout (using its own generated KV cache), while the teacher is evaluated at the same denoising states but uses a "cleaner AR-consistent temporal cache" by replacing older history with real video context. This provides dense, trajectory-level corrective targets for the student without modifying its final inference architecture, sampler, or cache mechanism.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This method provides a clear, post-training pathway to significantly enhance the long-term temporal coherence, visual quality, and motion dynamics of production-ready few-step autoregressive video generators without incurring any additional inference cost or latency. It addresses a critical pain point of error accumulation in long video rollouts, making it invaluable for improving user experience in applications requiring extended, high-quality generated video (e.g., interactive content, film pre-visualization, synthetic data generation). Teams can apply this fine-tuning strategy to existing fast models to reliably produce much longer, higher-quality videos.
