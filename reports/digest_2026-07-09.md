### Selective Timestep Weighting and Advantage-Based Replay for Sample-Efficient Diffusion RLHF
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07693v1)

**The TL;DR:** This paper significantly improves the feedback efficiency of Diffusion RLHF by dynamically weighting informative denoising timesteps and employing a prioritized replay mechanism for trajectories, leading to faster and cheaper alignment of generative models.

**Architectural Innovation:** While not modifying the core neural network architecture of diffusion models (e.g., U-Net, DiT), this work introduces innovations in the *RLHF optimization algorithm* for training these architectures. Specifically, it involves:
1.  A **per-timestep weighting scheme** that dynamically scales gradient contributions from different denoising steps during policy optimization, thereby influencing how the diffusion model's parameters are updated based on their importance.
2.  An **advantage-based replay mechanism** that intelligently reuses past high-value trajectories during training, reducing the need for new sample generation and reward evaluations.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This work directly addresses the primary bottleneck of high cost and time in aligning production-grade diffusion models using RLHF. Engineers can leverage these techniques to:
*   **Accelerate Model Alignment:** Achieve desired model quality and alignment with human preferences significantly faster, reducing development cycles from weeks to days for fine-tuning diffusion models (e.g., for custom styles, safety, or specific aesthetic criteria).
*   **Reduce Operational Costs:** Drastically cut down on human annotation costs or reward model inference expenses, making RLHF for diffusion models economically viable for a wider range of applications.
*   **Enable Feedback-Scarce Applications:** Implement RLHF in scenarios where obtaining human feedback is inherently rare, slow, or expensive, broadening the applicability of preference-based fine-tuning for specialized GenAI products.
*   **Improve Model Quality for Fixed Resources:** For a given budget of human feedback or computational resources, these methods allow the model to learn more effectively, resulting in higher-quality generations or better alignment outcomes.

**Architectural Innovation:** The core innovation is the integration of a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) framework into a Diffusion Transformer (DiT)-based video model, replacing traditional dense layers to optimize for a better balance between modeling capacity and inference efficiency.

**Engineering Takeaway:**
*   **Improved Inference Efficiency for Specialized Tasks:** Implementing MoE within DiT-like architectures can significantly reduce inference compute requirements for video generation, especially crucial for real-time applications in robotics or embodied AI where latency and power consumption are critical.

### Does Bielik Know What It Doesn't Know? Activation Dispersion Separates Entity Familiarity from Factual Reliability Across Model Scale
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07670v1)

**The TL;DR:** Large Language Models exhibit an internal signal in their MLP layer activations that reliably indicates their familiarity with an entity before generating an answer, which is distinct from their actual factual accuracy.

**Architectural Innovation:** No modifications to existing LLM architectures (e.g., U-Net, latent spaces, attention mechanisms) are proposed. Instead, the innovation lies in identifying and quantifying a diagnostic signal *within* the *post-SwiGLU MLP activations* of standard Transformer models using unsupervised dispersion measures (inverse participation ratio and spectral entropy), revealing an inherent property of these architectural components.

**Engineering Takeaway:**
This discovery enables a rapid, unsupervised, and zero-shot method to assess an LLM's entity familiarity *before* answer generation, with high reliability (AUROC 0.95-1.00) and minimal inference cost (single forward pass). This can be directly applied in production GenAI pipelines to:
1.  **Proactive Hallucination Prevention:** Immediately flag incoming prompts referencing entities the model is unfamiliar with (e.g., fabricated names or obscure concepts) *before* expensive full inference, routing them to human review, RAG pipelines, or specialized knowledge bases to prevent the generation of confidently false information.
2.  **Dynamic Refusal/Abstention Mechanisms:** Implement an automated refusal strategy for queries about unfamiliar entities, as the models themselves rarely abstain despite this internal "awareness," thereby enhancing system reliability and trustworthiness.
3.  **Cost-Effective Quality Control:** Reduce unnecessary computation by identifying and short-circuiting queries likely to lead to "unknown entity" hallucinations at the earliest possible stage, optimizing inference costs while improving output quality.
4.  **Enhanced Risk Scoring:** Integrate this familiarity signal into a broader confidence or risk score for generated content, particularly for applications sensitive to factual accuracy regarding named entities.
