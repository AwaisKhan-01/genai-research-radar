### Native Active Perception as Reasoning for Omni-Modal Understanding
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19341v1)

**The TL;DR:** OmniAgent is a novel interactive agent that uses a POMDP-based iterative Observation-Thought-Action cycle to selectively process long video and audio data, significantly reducing computational cost while improving understanding.

**Architectural Innovation:** The innovation is an "omni-modal agent" architecture that frames video understanding as a POMDP-based iterative Observation-Thought-Action cycle, fundamentally shifting from "watch-it-all" paradigms. This agent executes on-demand actions to selectively distill audio-visual cues into a persistent textual memory. It's operationalized through (1) Agentic Supervised Fine-Tuning (using best-of-N trajectory synthesis with dual-stage quality control) to bootstrap active perception, and (2) Agentic Reinforcement Learning with TAURA (Turn-aware Adaptive Uncertainty Rescaled Advantage) for credit assignment based on turn-level entropy.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This approach offers critical efficiency gains for production Generative AI pipelines dealing with long-form video content, as it effectively decouples reasoning complexity and computational cost from raw video duration. The ability of a 7B agent to outperform a 10x larger 72B model (Qwen2.5-VL-72B) demonstrates substantial potential for reduced inference costs, lower power consumption, and improved throughput. This makes it highly valuable for applications like automated surveillance, long-form meeting summarization, content moderation, or real-time event detection, where processing efficiency and scalable performance (due to positive test-time scaling) are paramount.

### Do as I Do: Dexterous Manipulation Data from Everyday Human Videos
[Read Full Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19333v1)

**The TL;DR:** This work presents an algorithm that transforms everyday human videos into scalable, robot-executable manipulation data for dexterous multi-fingered hands by reconstructing human-object interactions and retargeting them to robots.

**Architectural Innovation:** The core innovation is an **algorithmic pipeline** called DO AS I DO, designed for **end-to-end data generation** for dexterous robotics. This pipeline features a novel **hand-object interaction reconstruction module** capable of processing diverse monocular RGB video (egocentric/exocentric, in-the-wild) and a subsequent **embodiment-aware retargeting mechanism** that translates the reconstructed human motions into robot-executable actions, bridging the human-to-robot gap. This architecture is specifically engineered to overcome the challenges of scalable, real-world manipulation data creation from unstructured video.

**Engineering Takeaway:** This system offers a game-changing method for **massively scaling training data acquisition** for generative AI models in robotics, particularly for complex dexterous manipulation. Engineers can leverage this to:
1.  **Accelerate Policy Development:** Rapidly generate diverse manipulation trajectories from readily available human videos, significantly reducing the manual effort and cost of programming or simulating new robotic tasks.
2.  **Enhance Model Robustness:** Train GenAI control policies on a much larger and more varied dataset of real-world human demonstrations, leading to more generalized, robust, and higher-quality generated actions for robotic agents.
3.  **Democratize Data Collection:** Turn everyday video into a primary source of high-quality training data, enabling more accessible and cost-effective development of advanced robotic capabilities without specialized hardware or expert operators.
